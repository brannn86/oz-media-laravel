What this site measures

To run the station's site and player and to see what our audience listens to, watches and reads, we record the following. We don't sell it, use it for advertising or share it with anyone other than the providers that host the site for us.

Listening and watching. While the player is playing, it tells our server once a minute. For each listening or viewing session we keep the station, radio or video, when it started and ended, how many minutes it lasted, and the kind of device, operating system and browser (read from what your browser reports about itself).

A random ID for your browser. To count unique listeners, the player keeps a random ID in your browser's local storage (oz-visitor-id). It isn't linked to your name. If you are signed in, your account is used instead. Our server stores only a one-way hash of either, never the ID itself. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes the ID.

How you found us. The name of the website that sent you here (not the full address of the page) and any campaign tags in the link you followed (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign). They are kept in your browser for the visit and saved with your listening session.

Reading and reactions. How far down a news article readers scroll and how long it is open, and how many people react to a post. These are counted as totals per article and day, not per person.

The radio stream. Like any server, our streaming server sees the IP address and player of each listener while they are connected. We keep only listener counts and the kinds of players used, not addresses.

Page visits. We count page visits with Umami, a privacy-focused analytics tool that we host ourselves. It does not use cookies or follow you across other sites.

How long we keep it. Listening sessions and the daily records of unique listeners are deleted automatically after 13 months. Totals with nothing that identifies a person (such as listeners per minute) are kept for comparing years.

Your rights. Under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU No. 27 Tahun 2022) you can ask what personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and withdraw your consent. Contact us using the details on this site.
