Your account

If you create an account, we keep your name, email address and password (stored scrambled, so not even we can read it), and ask for your age range, gender and where you live. You can choose "Prefer not to say" for age and gender. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google instead of a password.

We use your age range, gender and location only to understand our audience as a whole (for example, how many listeners are 18 to 24, or live in Kota Bandung), to plan our programmes and to show advertisers totals, never your details. Your account also holds your OZ Poin, song requests, chat messages and rewards.

You can change these details on your profile page at any time, and delete your account there too, which removes them.
